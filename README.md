# Sakila Movie Rental Analysis | Power BI Project

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Sakila Movie Rental database using Excel, SQL, Power BI, and DAX to uncover business insights related to revenue, customer behavior, film performance, inventory trends, staff operations, and geographic revenue distribution.

The project includes interactive dashboards, KPI analysis, and business reporting to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Tools & Technologies Used

- **Excel** — Data Cleaning & Preparation
- **SQL** — Data Validation & Querying
- **Power BI** — Data Visualization & Dashboard Development
- **DAX** — KPI Measures & Business Calculations

---

## Dashboard Sections

- Executive Dashboard
- Customer & Sales Analysis
- Film & Inventory Analysis
- Staff & Store Performance
- Geographic Revenue Analysis

---

## Key Insights

- India generated the highest rental revenue
- Online payments contributed the highest sales share
- Active customers drove the majority of total revenue
- Action was the top-performing film category
- Revenue increased significantly during peak months
